# Problem 3.10

**Source**: Chapter 3, Problem 10

## Problem Statement

10. (a) The probability current density $\mathbf{j}(\mathbf{r}, t)$ is given in terms of the wave function by
    $$
    \mathbf{j}(\mathbf{r}, t) = \frac{1}{2m} \left[ \psi^*(\mathbf{r}, t) \frac{\hbar}{i}\nabla \psi(\mathbf{r}, t) - \frac{\hbar}{i}\nabla \psi^*(\mathbf{r}, t) \cdot \psi(\mathbf{r}, t) \right].
    $$
    Show directly from Schrödinger's equation that the probability density, $P(\mathbf{r}, t) = |\Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)|^2$ and the probability current density, obey the continuity equation
    $$
    \frac{\partial}{\partial t} P(\mathbf{r}, t) + \nabla \cdot \mathbf{j}(\mathbf{r}, t) = 0.
    $$
    Do not assume that the potential vanishes.
    (b) What is the form for $\mathbf{j}(\mathbf{r}, t)$ when there is a magnetic field present specified by the vector potential $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{r}, t)$?
