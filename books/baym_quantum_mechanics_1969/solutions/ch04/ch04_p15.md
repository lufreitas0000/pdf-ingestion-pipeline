# Problem 4.15

**Source**: Chapter 4, Problem 15

## Problem Statement

15. Estimate the zero point energy for a particle of mass $m$ in the following potentials:
(a) $V(x) = v x^4$ in one dimension, where $v$ is a positive constant.
(b) $V(r) = -e^2/r$ in three dimensions; this is the potential felt by an electron in a hydrogen atom. Express the estimate in eV, taking $e$ and $m$ to be the charge and mass of the electron.
(c) $V(r) = -q^2 e^{-kr}/r$ in three dimensions. Take $k = 1.3 \times 10^6 \text{ cm}^{-1}$ and $q^2 = e^2/14$. This problem requires a graphical solution. A potential of this form, called a "screened Coulomb potential," is the type felt by an electron due to an impurity of charge $e$ in a semiconductor such as germanium.
