# Problem 1.16

**Source**: Chapter 1, Problem 16

## Problem Statement

16. The trace of an $n \times n$ matrix is defined as the sum of its diagonal components, i.e., if
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\ a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \\ a_{n1} & a_{n2} & \dots & a_{nn} \end{pmatrix}
$$
then
$$
\text{tr } A = a_{11} + a_{22} + a_{33} + \dots + a_{nn}.
$$
Show that the trace of a matrix is independent of the basis in which the matrix is written, i.e., that it is invariant under a unitary transformation of the matrix,
$$
A \to U^\dagger A U,
$$
where U is unitary. Show that $\text{tr } AB = \text{tr } BA$.
